Data Deck

Mobile app prototype

Play through the app on the phone. Everything you can see can be tapped: tiles, charts, cards, badges, log rows. Sample data only.

  • Sign in and confirm your age
  • Pick a data source and set goals
  • Open the weekly pack
  • Finish a quest and level up
  • Lend a card to a study
  • Receive a daily payout
  • Share one answer with a second study
  • Stop sharing and see the request refused
  • Cash out
  • Log out and back in

Demo clock: one day passes every 10 seconds while a card is on loan, matching the server.
