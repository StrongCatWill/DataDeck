# AuthButton

Sign in with Apple, Google or email (a 6-digit code, no password). A wallet is created in the background.

- On press the label becomes a spinner and "Signing in…".
- Terms and Privacy notice are links that open a short sheet.
- After logging out the same screen reads "Welcome back" and returns straight to Today.
- The birth-year stepper follows first sign-in. Under 18, Continue stays disabled.
