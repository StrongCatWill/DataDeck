# Button

A chunky button with a darker "lip" underneath. Pressing moves it 3px down onto the lip, with a light haptic.

- `dd-btn dd-primary` (`sleep` on `sleep-d` lip): the one main action on a screen. Sits at the bottom, full width (`dd-wide`).
- `dd-btn dd-go` (`steps-d`): the end of a set-up step, "Start playing".
- `dd-btn`: secondary, white with a `line` outline and `lip`.
- `dd-btn dd-danger`: only for stopping a share or deleting the account.
- `dd-btn dd-ghost`: "Not now", "Don't allow", "Skip". No lip.
- `dd-small`: inside rows (Connect, View).
- Minimum height 50px (38px small). Label says what happens: "Lend for 30 days", never "Submit".
- Disabled is 45% opacity and does not move.
