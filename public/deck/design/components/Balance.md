# Balance

The Wallet header: a slowly spinning 3D coin, the balance, 30 payout bars and a Cash out button.

- The coin (`dd-coin3`) is five stacked discs, 8px thick, rotating once every 4.5 seconds.
- One solid bar per paid day. The number counts up when a payout arrives.
- Three stat tiles underneath (earned in total, days on loan, studies) open "Where it came from".
- A pill states "1 USDC is about 1 US dollar". No other crypto words.
