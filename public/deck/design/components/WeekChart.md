# WeekChart

Seven labelled bars drawn as 3D blocks (a lighter top face and a darker side face), a dashed goal line and "n of 7 on target".

- Each bar is a button. Tapping it outlines the bar and writes that day under the chart: "Thursday: 9.0, on target".
- Days that missed the goal are 35% opacity. Nothing turns red; the chart shows what happened and does not judge it.
- Used on card detail, the Steps screen and the Sleep screen.
- On card detail it is followed by "Why you got this card": the exact rules, each with a check.
