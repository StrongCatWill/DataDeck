# ConsentSheet

The bottom sheet where a loan is agreed. It is the calmest screen in the app: white, flat, no game effects.

1. Who is asking and "Pseudonymised".
2. The question as a title: "Lend Deep Sleeper to Trinity Sleep Lab?"
3. The summary box, labelled **AI-generated summary**. It shimmers, then types in. The main button stays disabled until it has finished.
4. **What leaves your phone**: three icon tiles, shared types ticked, the others crossed.
5. Four facts with icons.
6. "Read the researcher's full notice". Opening it earns the "Read a full notice" badge.
7. The action ("Lend for 30 days", "Share one answer") and "Not now".

- If the pack is unopened the action becomes "Open my pack first". If the card isn't held, the action is replaced by an explanation and "How to earn it".
- Confirming asks for Face ID. No pre-ticked boxes, no countdowns, no XP.
