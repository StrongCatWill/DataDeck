# CoverFlow

After the pack is opened, Today shows the week's cards in a horizontal 3D carousel.

- Scroll-snap to the centre. Cards rotate around the vertical axis by their distance from the centre (up to 42 degrees) and shrink slightly, so the middle card faces you.
- Tapping a card opens its detail. "See all" opens the Deck tab.
- With Reduce Motion the cards stay flat. The preview here shows the flat state.
