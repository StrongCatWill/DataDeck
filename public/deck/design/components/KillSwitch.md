# KillSwitch

The control that stops a loan. Press and hold for `dur-hold` (1200ms) while a dark fill sweeps across the button; letting go early cancels.

- Label: "Hold to stop sharing", then "Keep holding…".
- Haptics: a tick every 200ms, then one heavy tap.
- Always followed by: "Stops future access and payments right away. Batches already shared can't be taken back." and the two-tap alternative.
- The Sharing stopped sheet shows three outcomes: **Stopped**, **Destroyed**, **Can't be taken back**, and adds "Your level, cards and streak are untouched."
- Afterwards the loan screen shows an "Erasure request sent" row that opens a three-step tracker.
- Never say "deleted forever".
