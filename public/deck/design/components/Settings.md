# Settings

- **Setting row** (`dd-setrow`): icon badge, name, one line of explanation and a toggle.
- **Menu row** (`dd-row`): the same with a chevron, for screens.
- **Log out** (`dd-btn dd-outline-danger`): the last item on You. Its sheet says what stays ("Your cards, level, streak and wallet stay on your account") and, if a loan is active, warns that it keeps running and offers "Manage the loan first".
- Delete account is separate, under "Export or delete my data", and needs two taps.
