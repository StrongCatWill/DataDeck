# BountyRow

One study on the Bounties tab: an icon badge, its name, what it wants, how long, how long it keeps the data, and a flat price.

- Matching row (`dd-row dd-m`): `sky` outline and "You have this card". 
- Status pills replace the match pill once used: "On loan", "Stopped", "Answered".
- Every row opens the consent sheet, including studies whose card the player doesn't hold; there the sheet explains what is missing and links to how to earn the card.
- Three kinds are shown: a 30-day daily loan, a single answer, and a 24-hour copy.
- Copy: lend, borrow, share. Never sell.
