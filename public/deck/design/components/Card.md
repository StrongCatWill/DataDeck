# Card

A collectible card for one week of data: an icon emblem, the stat that earned it and a seven-day mini chart.

| Card | Rarity | Class | Colourway | Text |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Early Bird | Common, 1 gem | `dd-early` | `#FFB05C` to `#FF7A45`, edge `#C2491C` | `night` |
| Deep Sleeper | Rare, 2 gems | `dd-sleep` | `sleep` to `sleep-d`, edge `#35208F` | white |
| Marathon Week | Epic, 3 gems | `dd-marathon` | `#5BE3A8` to `#22BF7C`, edge `steps-d` | `night` |
| Perfect Week | Legendary, 4 gems | `dd-perfect dd-foil` | `#FFD25A` to `#FFA51F`, edge `#B87300` | `night` |

- Locked cards (`dd-lock3`) sit in the same grid with a dashed outline, their name and progress. Tapping one explains how to earn it.
- Collection rows (`dd-collrow`) list all six cards with how many times each was found.
- Rarity is printed as a word beside the gems. Flags: "1 match", "On loan", "Stopped", "Answered".
