# LevelHero

The top of the Today tab: a top bar (avatar, greeting, notification bell with an unread count, balance chip) and the level hero.

- The level sits in a 3D gold hexagon (`dd-lvl3`): a darker copy 6px below gives it thickness, and it bobs gently.
- Beside it: the level name, "70 XP to Pacer", the streak pill, the XP bar and seven day dots.
- Everything is tappable: avatar opens You, the bell opens Notifications, the chip opens Wallet, the badge and name open the Levels sheet, the streak pill and dots open the Streak sheet.
- Level names: Rookie, Walker, Night Owl, Pacer, Legend. Cosmetic only; a level never changes what a study pays.
- XP comes only from habits and collecting. Lending gives no XP, badge or streak.
