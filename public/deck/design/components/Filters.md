# Filters

- **Segmented control** (`dd-seg`): two views of one tab, e.g. "This week" and "Collection" on Deck.
- **Filter chips** (`dd-fchip`): one row that scrolls sideways. The pressed chip is `night` with white text. Bounties uses All, For my cards, Sleep, Steps, Heart.
- **Explainer** (`dd-steps3`): three icon tiles that explain a flow in a glance, e.g. "How lending works". The whole strip opens Help.
