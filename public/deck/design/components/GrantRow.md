# GrantRow

A loan the player has agreed to. In the Wallet it is a row; opened, it shows a countdown ring and a batch grid.

- Countdown ring: `gold` on `gold-t`, days left in the middle.
- Batch grid (`dd-dot30`): 30 squares, one a day. Sent days are gold with a check and open that batch's record; tomorrow's has a gold outline.
- "What I agreed to" opens the consent record, which can be saved as a copy.
- The kill switch sits directly under the facts, above the grid, so it is never hidden below the fold.
