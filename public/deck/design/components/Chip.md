# Chip

Small pills that label a state. The word always carries the meaning; colour only supports it.

- `dd-pill dd-gold`: streak, XP reward, "On loan".
- `dd-pill dd-red`: "Stopped" only.
- `dd-pill dd-sky`: a study matches a card you hold.
- `dd-pill dd-sleep`: the "AI-generated summary" label.
- `dd-pill dd-steps`: connected or on.
- `dd-pill`: neutral facts on a bounty (duration, retention, "Pseudonymised").
- `dd-coinchip`: the USDC balance in the top-right of Deck, Bounties and detail screens. It bumps (`ease-snap`) when a payout lands.
