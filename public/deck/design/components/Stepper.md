# Stepper

A minus button, a large value and a plus button, used for the birth year and for daily goals.

- Steps move in 500s (3,000 to 15,000); sleep in half hours (6 to 10).
- A caption translates the number: "About 55 minutes of walking", "In bed by 11:00 pm for a 7:00 am start".
- Goals are asked once in first run and can be changed on the Steps and Sleep screens or in You. Lowering a goal never costs XP or the streak.
