# StatTile

The live infographics on the Today tab, laid out as a two-column bento grid with no empty cells.

- **Steps** (`dd-tile dd-steps dd-tall`): a ring with a white end cap, the count, "to go", then twelve hourly bars with the busiest slot solid. Opens the Steps screen.
- **Sleep** (`dd-tile dd-sleep`): last night's total and the week's bars with a dashed goal line. Opens the Sleep screen.
- **Resting heart** (`dd-tile dd-heart`): the number and a pulse line. Only with a watch source. Opens the Heart screen.
- **Sleep mode** (`dd-tile dd-gold`): replaces the heart tile for the phone tracker. Opens the Sleep mode sheet.
- **Closest card** (`dd-nextcard`): a tilted locked card, its name and progress towards it. Opens "How to earn it".
- Every tile is a button. A small chevron is not needed; the whole tile presses down 2px.
