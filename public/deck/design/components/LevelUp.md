# LevelUp

A full-screen moment when XP passes 400: slow rays, the 3D level badge spinning in once, confetti, and one button.

- The copy always repeats the rule: "Levels are for fun: every player gets the same price from a study."
- Haptic: a short rising pattern. With Reduce Motion the badge fades in and there is no confetti.
- The Levels sheet lists all five levels, marks "You are here", and states what gives XP and what never does (lending).
