# CashOut

A sheet that sends the whole balance to the player.

- Below 1.00 USDC it explains the minimum and offers "See bounties".
- Two options as radio rows: "Bank account" (default, in euro, 1 to 2 working days) and "My own wallet" (marked Advanced).
- Face ID confirms. The success state shows the large 3D coin and "x USDC is on its way".
- The activity log gets a "Cashed out" line with a minus amount.
