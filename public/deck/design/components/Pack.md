# Pack

The sealed weekly pack is a 3D foil pouch: a front face with a gold seal, a darker side, crimped ends and a soft shadow on the ground.

- It floats up and down slowly, a light sweep crosses the foil, and it tilts towards the pointer.
- Tap to open: it shakes, lifts and drops away (`dur-slow`); cards deal in, confetti falls once, "+40 XP".
- On Today it appears as a banner with a miniature of the same pack.
- Under the pack, three small tiles show what it was built from (nights, steps, resting heart rate); each opens that data screen.
