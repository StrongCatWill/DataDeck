# LogRow

One line of the activity log. Every line is a button that opens its record: what, when, amount and the public record reference with a Copy button.

- Sent: green badge and the payout. Stop: red badge. Refused: red lock and bold "Request refused." Cash out: bank icon and a minus amount.
- The record sheet repeats: "The public record holds a random ID, a time and an amount. Never your health data."
